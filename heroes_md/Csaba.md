# Hero: Csaba

Class inspiration: Rogue
Player inspiration: Heavy smoker, theoretical chemist (math focus, dynamics)
Playstyle: Combo, Midrange

### Hero power:

Mana cost: 2
Effect: Give a 0 cost spell card named "Cigi" to the player's hand with a random effect from a pool.
Effect pool:

- Give a minion -2 HP
- Deal 2 damage to your champion, draw a card
- Deal 1 dmg to a minion and to a random neighbouring one
- Return a minion you control to your own hand.
- Summon a 1/1 "Cigi" minion
